NEXNOVA

VITS an esteemed and highly active organization at St. Sylvester's College, known for its prominence among Kandy schools, is delighted to announce the upcoming Annual ICT Day. This year, we aim to host an exceptional event, bringing together students from the leading schools across Sri Lanka. We are committed to ensuring a memorable and successful ICT Day, celebrating the spirit of technology and knowledge-sharing among students, even amidst challenging times. With the vision of creating a brighter future for our students and the nation, we eagerly anticipate the upcoming Annual ICT Day and seek the College's permission and support to continue making a lasting impact in the field of ICT and education.

EVENT HIGHLIGHT

  • To educate participant about Networking and Future.
  • invite participants to improve their knowledge.
  • Make participants aware of ICT in Future.
  • Quiz Competition for Entertainment.
