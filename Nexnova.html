NEXNOVA

NEXNOVA is the annual ICT day of St. Sylvester's College Kandy. It is organized by the ICT Society of the college. The event is organized to showcase the talents of the students in the field of ICT and to provide a platform for them to showcase their skills. The event includes a variety of competitions and workshops that are designed to help students develop their skills in the field of ICT. The event also includes a number of guest speakers who are experts in the field of ICT and who can provide valuable insights and advice to the students.
